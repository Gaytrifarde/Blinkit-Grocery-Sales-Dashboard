# 🛒 Blinkit Grocery Delivery & Sales Performance Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze Blinkit’s grocery sales, customer behavior, product performance, revenue trends, and delivery efficiency.

The dashboard transforms transactional order data into meaningful business insights using interactive KPIs, charts, filters, and visual analytics.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Quick-commerce businesses need to continuously monitor **sales, customer behavior, product demand, and delivery performance** to understand overall business health.

This project provides an executive-level view of Blinkit’s performance through a single interactive dashboard.

The dashboard helps users:

* Monitor overall sales and order performance
* Analyze monthly revenue trends
* Identify top-performing products
* Understand customer purchasing behavior
* Track repeat customers
* Evaluate delivery performance
* Analyze product demand
* Compare orders and revenue
* Filter data dynamically for deeper analysis

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 📈 Sales & Revenue Analysis

The dashboard analyzes sales performance through monthly revenue trends and product-level revenue analysis.

**Monthly Revenue Trend**

The line chart shows how revenue changes throughout the year and helps identify:

* High-performing months
* Low-performing months
* Revenue fluctuations
* Seasonal patterns

**Top Products by Revenue**

The product revenue chart highlights products contributing significantly to overall revenue.

Examples include:

* Vitamins
* Pet Treats
* Cough Syrup
* Toilet Cleaner

---

### 👥 Customer Analysis

Customer behavior is analyzed using total customer count and repeat-customer percentage.

The **Repeat Customer %** indicates the proportion of customers who placed more than one order.

This metric provides an understanding of recurring customer activity and purchasing behavior.

---

### 🚚 Delivery Performance

The dashboard evaluates delivery efficiency by analyzing delivery-status categories.

| Delivery Status           | Percentage | Revenue |
| ------------------------- | ---------: | ------: |
| **On-Time**               |     69.42% |  ₹3.45M |
| **Slightly Delayed**      |     20.81% |  ₹1.03M |
| **Significantly Delayed** |      9.77% |  ₹0.49M |

A combination of **gauge and donut charts** provides a clear view of delivery performance.

---

### 🛍️ Product Demand Analysis

The dashboard uses a scatter plot to analyze the relationship between **order volume and revenue**.

This helps identify:

* High-demand products
* High-revenue products
* Products with high orders but relatively lower revenue
* Potential product outliers

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters
